📖 How does "going to" work?

We use going to to talk about future plans and predictions — things we think will happen.

am / is / are + going to + verb
QuestionAre you going to win the lottery?
Yes 👍Yes, I am. I'm going to buy a house!
No 👎No, I'm not going to win. I never play!

🎮 How to play

1. A fortune card appears. The current player reads the prediction and turns it into a question.

2. The other player answers (Yes or No) and adds one extra sentence.

3. Then it's the other player's turn! Take turns until all cards are done. 🔄
